May 2024 Gannon Superstorm — MHD-corrected density hindcast

G5 · Dst −412 ⚠ PLACEHOLDER DATA

A 72-hour replay (10 May 12:00 UTC → 13 May 12:00 UTC) of the first G5 storm since 2003. Empirical density models drove the thermosphere from a 3-hour Ap index that pegged at its 400 ceiling for ~24 h on May 11 — they could not see the storm climbing past the cap. This hindcast replaces saturated Ap with two surrogates: one from a BATS-R-US replay of the L1 IMF, one reconstructed from ground magnetometers. Both surrogates are validated against GRACE-FO and Swarm-C accelerometer truth.

Methodology

L1 IMF (OMNI HRO 1-min) is replayed into BATS-R-US under SWMF to yield ΦPC and HPI at 5-minute cadence. ΦPC and HPI drive an OLS regression against the GFZ historical Ap to produce a non-saturating pseudo-Ap series; a parallel regression is fit from ground-magnetometer SME and a Newell-Gjerloev-style Joule-heating proxy. NRLMSISE-00 is then evaluated at 400 km under each Ap track. Validation uses TU Delft GRACE-FO v02 and ESA Swarm-C accelerometer-derived density.

Full reproducibility steps in the Gannon runbook and design rationale in the simulation design doc.
