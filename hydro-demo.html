A 2-D log-polar FARGO kernel written in Rust, compiled to WebAssembly, advancing live in your browser. This is step one toward a real 3-D protoplanetary-disc solver — first prove the toolchain, then layer on the vertical structure and the magnetic field.

Method

Locally isothermal log-polar grid · PLM reconstruction with minmod limiter · LLF/Rusanov Riemann flux · SSP-RK2 (Shu-Osher) in time · FARGO azimuthal shift (integer + fractional, single-shot per substep) · explicit pressure / centrifugal / planetary-gravity sources · de Val-Borro (2006) wave-killing boundaries. Formally O(h², dt²); drift converges as h² in the transient regime, plateaus at the damping-zone floor in steady-state. HLLC + SIMD next.
