Eclipse Calendar
Computed from the node geometry (Meeus ch. 54), not a list: the Moon's orbit is tilted 5.1°, so an eclipse needs a new or full moon while the Moon is within ~21° of a node — most months miss. Times are UTC, greatest eclipse, ±minutes. Solar ground paths are beyond this theory — check a local source before travelling. Gated by tests/moon-ephemeris.mjs.

Orbital Evolution — Deep Time
The Moon is spiralling away at 3.83 cm/yr (laser-ranged off the Apollo reflectors), and Earth's spin pays for it. Scrub 4.5 billion years — distance follows the geological record (tidal rhythmites), and everything else is derived: the day length falls out of angular-momentum conservation and lands on the measured paleo-days on its own. Atmosphere — a Collisionless Exosphere
The Moon's entire atmosphere weighs a few tonnes — atoms hop ballistically and essentially never collide. Non-condensable gas pools on the cold night side; argon freezes out overnight and returns in a pre-dawn bulge; sodium glows sunward and streams into a tail. Drawing it at all is the exaggeration — to the eye it is invisible.
