Methods & provenance

Which system is this, really?

The viral description — “the most massive pair of black holes ever found, 60 billion solar masses, 4.4 billion light-years away, in a dark core devoid of starlight” — refers to Abell 402-BCG (McDonald et al. 2026, ApJL 1002, L19), where a 60 ± 20 ×10⁹ M☉ candidate binary is one of two allowed interpretations of a kiloparsec-scale starless cavity. Abell 85’s central galaxy Holm 15A hosts a single ultramassive black hole — (4.0±0.8)×10¹⁰ M☉ from axisymmetric modeling (Mehrgan et al. 2019), revised to 2.16×10¹⁰ M☉ with triaxial modeling (Liepold, Ma & Walsh 2025) — whose record-sized diffuse core is the aftermath of binary scouring. This lab therefore runs Holm 15A as a reconstructed history (the pair that made today’s giant), the Abell 402-BCG pair forward from today, and the genuinely confirmed record pair B2 0402+379 (2.8×10¹⁰ M☉ combined at 7.3 pc; Surti, Romani et al. 2024) as a stalled control.

What is physically modeled

  • Staged binary evolution — the Begelman–Blandford–Rees (1980) sequence. Approach: Chandrasekhar (1943) dynamical friction, isothermal-sphere closed form. Hard binary: three-body slingshot hardening d(1/a)/dt = H·Gρ/σ (Quinlan 1996, H≈16) scaled by a loss-cone refill factor — the final-parsec dial (Vasiliev, Antonini & Merritt 2015: ≈1 for triaxial nuclei, ≪1 for spherical ones). GW inspiral: Peters (1964) orbit-averaged ⟨da/dt⟩, ⟨de/dt⟩, summed continuously with the stellar term. The engine is validated against the closed-form Peters coalescence time to <2% in tests/abell85-physics.mjs.
  • Merger — NR-calibrated fits: radiated energy ≈4.4–4.8% Mc² and remnant spin a_f≈0.69 at q=1 (Hofmann-type η-fits); recoil kick from the González et al. (2007) mass-asymmetry formula, with a superkick option standing in for in-plane spins (Campanelli et al. 2007; Lousto & Zlochower 2011). The kicked remnant executes a damped oscillation through the core (Gualandris & Merritt 2008).
  • Star cluster — N test particles (your choice, 2k–16k) in the analytic Dehnen (1993) host potential plus the two moving holes, integrated with kick–drift–kick leapfrog and per-frame substepping. Slingshot ejections are real three-body dynamics; the ejected-mass counter is the measured core-scouring deficit, shown against the semi-analytic expectation (~0.5 M_bin per merger; Merritt 2006). Watch ρ(r) flatten and β(r) go tangential inside the carved core — the two observational fingerprints of scouring (Thomas et al. 2014; measured in Holm 15A by Mehrgan et al. 2019).
  • Gravitational waves — quadrupole frequency and sky-averaged strain at the true source distances; the track is plotted across the pulsar-timing band with the NANOGrav 15-yr amplitude marked (Agazie et al. 2023). A 10¹⁰·⁵–10¹⁰·⁸ M☉ binary merges inside the PTA band (~10⁻⁷ Hz) and never reaches LISA.
  • Lensing & shadows — the composite pass applies the thin-lens point-mass mapping β = θ − θ_E²/θ with the Einstein radius computed from the camera distance (θ_E·D = √(2r_s·D)) and an event-horizon shadow at the √27·GM/c² photon-capture radius — the size the EHT confirmed is spin-insensitive to ≲4% (EHT Collaboration 2019).

Live post-Newtonian endgame (Phase 2)

Inside a < 300 GM/c² the rendered binary stops being a Kepler ellipse driven by the orbit-averaged history and becomes a direct RK4 integration (≈240 adaptive substeps per orbit) of the harmonic-gauge PN equations of motion: the 1PN conservative terms (EIH; Blanchet, Living Rev. Relativ. 17, 2) that produce the orbit-by-orbit periapsis rosette drawn behind the holes, and the 2.5PN Burke–Thorne radiation-reaction drag whose orbit average reproduces Peters (1964). The live-state panel shows the direct integration against the orbit-averaged theory in real time: osculating a (PN) vs the Peters track, measured Δϖ per orbit vs 6πGM/(c²a(1−e²)), and cumulative ΔE vs the Peters–Mathews GW luminosity (which doubles as the integrator-quality readout). The validation contract lives in tests/abell85-physics.mjs: Kepler energy conservation |ΔE/E| ≈ 10⁻⁷ over 40 orbits, 1PN apsidal advance within 0.5% of theory, 2.5PN decay within 0.4% of Peters. 2PN conservative terms are deliberately excluded until they can be validated against an independent result — they are a few-percent precession correction in this window. Use the 0.5–5 kyr/s speeds to watch individual final orbits; at faster timeline speeds the PN view hands back to Kepler rendering and says so.

Loss cone & star inspector (Phase 2)

Every bound star is classified each frame against the loss-cone boundary L_lc = √(2·G·M_bin·a) (Merritt): stars below it reach pericenters ≲ a and are next in line for the three-body slingshot — they render cyan. The loss-cone chart histograms L/L_lc for core stars with the boundary in red: watch the depletion notch form as the binary consumes its fodder, and the boundary itself collapse as a(t) decays. The test-particle cloud shows pure draining; collisional and triaxial refill is the physics the semi-analytic refill dial stands in for. Click any star to inspect it: specific energy, angular momentum, L/L_lc, radius, speed, and status, with a live trail on the selected star. Click empty space to release.

Mock observations (Phase 3)

The lab now observes itself the way a telescope would, so the simulation can be compared with the published measurements on their own terms (constant mass-to-light assumed):

  • Mock photometry — the live star field is projected along the line of sight into Σ(R); the cusp radius r_γ (where the projected log-slope steepens through −½, the Lauer/Carollo definition) is measured live and plotted against the observed value — Holm 15A's record core (r_γ = 4.11 kpc in the deep Mehrgan et al. 2019 data; 4.57 kpc in the original 2014 Nuker fit; core-Sérsic R_b ≈ 2.8 kpc) or Abell 402-BCG's 2.2 kpc flattened core. Watch scouring grow the mock measurement — and note that a single merger tops out near ~1 kpc, well short of 4.57 kpc: that shortfall is itself the published result (Nasim et al. 2021) that record cores demand repeated mergers of already-cored progenitors plus recoil sloshing, exactly the Rantala et al. scenario for Holm 15A.
  • Mock IFU kinematics — a MUSE/KCWI-style map of mean line-of-sight velocity (blueshift ← neutral → redshift, astronomy convention) plus an annular σ_LOS(R) profile; the central value is quoted against the measured dispersion (346 km/s for Holm 15A). The central Kepler rise near the holes is exactly the signal dynamical modelers fit for the black hole mass.
  • GW observatory — the strain–frequency chart carries a schematic PTA single-source sensitivity curve (anchored to the NANOGrav 15-yr scale, labeled approximate) and the live state reports a "PTA-resolvable?" verdict. The 🔊 button sonifies the chirp: audio frequency = f_GW × 3×10¹⁰ — the shift factor is displayed because the physical signal peaks near 10⁻⁷ Hz, thirty octaves below human hearing.

Controls

  • Orbit mode (default): drag to orbit the center, scroll to zoom. Presets fly the camera cinematically. Fly mode (F or the 🚀 button): WASD thrust + Q/E vertical in the view frame, Shift ×5 boost, drag to look, scroll adjusts the speed scale. Flight speed is distance-adaptive — proportional to your distance from the nearest black hole — so the same keys carry you from the 30 kpc cluster core down to the photon ring without a speed slider.
  • Space plays/pauses the timeline. The scale bar (bottom left) and the camera chip (top) always show the current physical scale, position, and speed.
  • Rendering is camera-relative ("floating origin"), so there is no float32 jitter even at 10⁻⁶ of the scene scale; the orbital-plane tilt slider inclines the binary against the equatorial scale rings to keep the scene visibly three-dimensional.

Honesty contract (what is NOT modeled)

  • The binary’s orbital elements follow the calibrated staged model, not a self-consistent N-body integration — the star field responds live but does not back-react. (That’s the restricted approximation; KETJU-class simulations do it self-consistently.)
  • Star-field mode chip: live = leapfrog-integrated; statistical = re-drawn from the carved density profile after a timeline jump or when playback outruns the honest step. The re-draw is seeded and deterministic.
  • Post-Newtonian dynamics appear as 1PN periapsis precession and the Peters 2.5PN-order decay; the final ~dozen orbits and ringdown are schematic (plunge at 6GM/c²), not an IMR waveform.
  • No gas: these are dry, gas-poor ellipticals — that is why their cores are dark. Relativistic ray tracing at horizon scale lives in the TON 618 observatory, not here.
  • Abell 402-BCG’s σ and stellar mass are not yet published; Holm 15A-like values are assumed and labeled. Distances: Holm 15A z=0.0555 (D_L≈250 Mpc); Abell 402 ~4.4 Gly (light-travel).
