Showcase
Re-form the Sun's planets
A 0.02 M☉ Hayashi protoplanetary disc evolves under Shakura–Sunyaev α-viscosity (α = 10⁻³). Eight embryos accrete pebbles, migrate, and collide. Around 50–100 Myr, Theia strikes proto-Earth; a circumterrestrial debris disc condenses into the Moon outside the Roche limit.
Physics: Lynden-Bell & Pringle (1974), Weidenschilling (1977), Lambrechts & Johansen (2012), Paardekooper, Baruteau & Kley (2010, 2011), Canup (2012), Ćuk & Stewart (2012), Kopparapu et al. (2013, 2014), Caldiroli et al. (2025).
