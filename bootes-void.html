The Boötes Void

Dynamical footprint A million cubic megaparsecs of almost nothing — and what its absence does to everything around it

The interesting question was never does a void have gravity. Of course it does. The question is whether the Boötes underdensity leaves a measurable dynamical signature in the cosmic web around it, and out to what radius it still matters. This page runs that chain end to end — density to integrated contrast to peculiar velocity to potential to gravity to tidal shear — and then performs the experiment that actually answers it: fill the void back in to cosmic mean density, leave everything else exactly as it was, and subtract the two universes.

A void does not push. There is no repulsive force anywhere in this. There is simply less inward attraction from the empty direction, so matter's motion on top of the Hubble flow is outward — and that outflow, the tidal squeeze that builds the void's own wall, the cold spot it prints on the microwave background and the shear it puts on background galaxies are all consequences of one missing 10¹⁶ M☉.

MODEL
What is measured and what is modelled. The void's position, redshift, size and galaxy deficit are published measurements (Kirshner et al. 1981, 1987), and the named clusters around it are transcribed from the literature at —. Everything else on this page is a model. The continuous density field is a fitted HSW profile, not a reconstruction; the filaments, nodes and galaxies you can see in the render are a seeded synthetic realisation constrained to carry the profile's own compensating-wall mass and to point towards the real cluster directions. Nobody has published a matter-density reconstruction of this region — that observation is the thing this page is arguing for. No number here is a detection.
WebGL is unavailable in this browser, so the 3D stage cannot draw. Every number the render illustrates is in the figures below — the physics does not live in the picture.

Test 2 — density against velocity identity

Density says where matter is missing. Velocity says how gravity responded. They are different measurements, and linear theory says they must agree:

∇·v = −a H f δ_m

The two curves are the same curve, normalised. That is not a fit — it is an identity, and tests/bootes-void-model.mjs verifies it in integral form (the divergence theorem) against a quadrature that shares no code with the kernel. For a real survey this is the strongest single consistency check available: if the observed velocity divergence matched the observed density deficit, the void would be confirmed as a dynamically evolving gravitational structure rather than a gap in sampling.

Test 3 — the tidal signature Level 2

A void does not build its wall by pushing. It squeezes radially while letting go tangentially, and matter flattens into the shell. That is a statement about the eigenvalues of the tidal tensor, and it has a clean closed form.

Test 4 — delete Boötes the experiment

Two universes. In A, the measured underdensity sits inside its clumped wall. In B, the underdensity is filled to cosmic mean and everything else is byte-for-byte identical — same wall, same seed, same particles. Subtract the gravity fields and what is left is the void's own contribution, exactly. Switch the stage to Δg to see it in three dimensions.

The web carries exactly the profile's own compensating-wall mass — nobody typed a cluster mass in, because any mass typed in would make "does the void or the filament dominate?" a question about what somebody typed. Particle radii are drawn from the profile's own δ⁺(r)r² measure, so the clumped model and the smooth model have identical enclosed-mass profiles and the only thing that differs between them is arrangement. The named clusters set where the mass concentrates, not how much of it there is.

How to read this page

  • Everything labelled identity is mathematics and is verified to machine precision by node tests/bootes-void-model.mjs.
  • Everything labelled fitted or model depends on the profile, and the profile depends on the bias slider. Move it and watch how much moves with it.
  • Everything labelled honest null is a prediction that current instruments cannot detect for a single void. That is the correct answer, and saying so is the point.
  • Lengths are comoving Mpc, never h⁻¹ Mpc. Velocities are peculiar, never recession. Accelerations are quoted in km/s per Gyr because 10⁻¹³ m/s² is not a number anyone can hold.

Kernels: js/bootes-void-model.js (physics) · js/bootes-web-model.js (the synthetic web) · js/bootes-void-data.js (the measured inputs). All three are pure — no DOM, no fetch, no ambient time — and gated by tests/bootes-void-model.mjs and tests/bootes-web-model.mjs.
