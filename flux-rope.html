What this is

Every forecast on this platform is driven by the solar wind's Bz — and observed L1 data gives only 30–60 minutes of physical lead time. This engine models the CME itself as a magnetized flux rope (a 3DCORE-class tapered torus with a Gold–Hoyle twisted field), propagates it Sun→Earth with drag-based kinematics, and synthesizes the Bz(t) a virtual spacecraft at L1 would measure — days before arrival, with honest probabilities from a real ensemble.

How it works

The physics runs in a Rust→WASM kernel in your browser — a 1,000-member ensemble computes in tens of milliseconds. The heliosphere view is a GLSL port of the same field equations (the kernel is the oracle). Equations and conventions: physics spec · validation is pinned by unit + hindcast gates in the repo.
