Storm Observatory

On 3 February 2022, SpaceX launched 49 Starlink satellites into a 210 km parking orbit — into the teeth of a storm rated only G1–G2, minor. The thermosphere thickened ~50 % more than models predicted, drag beat the ion thrusters, and 38 satellites reentered. Two years later the Gannon G5 superstorm heated the same atmosphere an order of magnitude harder.

This instrument replays both against the same 20,000-object LEO catalog, plus a scenario lane you can dial from quiet-sun to Carrington-class — superposed on storm-epoch time, physics in a bit-exact Rust→WASM drag kernel, every dial carrying its observational provenance.

Storm Observatory — methods

Three thermospheric density histories run against one LEO population, aligned on τ = t − t_peak (superposed-epoch time): Starlink Feb 2022 (real GFZ ap; the minor storm that killed 38 satellites), Gannon May 2024 (the G5 extreme), and a scenario lane — quiet Dec-2019 counterfactual by default, composable to Halloween-2003 (real ap) or a Carrington-class synthesis with intensity / onset / duration dials (log-space interpolation; α>1 is labeled extrapolation).

Density

Each lane carries an NRLMSISE-00 grid ρ(150–1000 km, t) baked offline (scripts/build-storm-bundle.mjs) with the event's REAL 7-element ap history per sample, from the same vendored Rust→WASM MSIS the Gannon hindcast page uses. Inputs are clamped to the model's validity envelope (recorded in provenance). The known MSIS storm underprediction — our tests measure 1.11× at 210 km where ~1.35–1.6× was observed in Feb 2022 — is deliberately on display: closing that gap with MHD-corrected drivers is this platform's core thesis.

Orbits & drag

  • Near-circular King-Hele decay da/dt = −√(μa)·ρ·B with ρ at perigee; apogee-first decay for e > 0.005 (the circularize-then-spiral phenomenology of the 210×340 km cohort); J2 secular Ω̇/ω̇; per-object adaptive substeps keyed on perigee altitude.
  • The race dial: continuous ion raise (m/s per day) vs storm drag on the 49-satellite cohort, plus the attitude call (passive tumble bc≈0.015 vs edge-on bc≈0.004). At quiet drag the raise wins; the storm erodes or kills the margin — that race is the Feb-2022 story.
  • Bit-exact engines: the Rust→WASM kernel (rust-storm) reproduces the JS reference to the last f64 bit over 72 h of integration (transcendentals quarantined by f32 quantization), so deterministic replay — scrub back, re-integrate, identical history — is a tested contract, not an aspiration.
  • Population: 20,000 objects — documented Starlink shells, the Group 4-7 injection cohort, ISS/Tiangong, the sun-synchronous ridge, and the Cosmos-2251 / Iridium-33 / Fengyun-1C clouds. Structure is real; individual objects are drawn from distributions until the CelesTrak GP re-bake (scripts/build-storm-catalog.mjs --gp) clears the watermark.

Honesty

Any placeholder input keeps the on-canvas watermark lit (the Gannon-page convention): currently the 81-day F10.7 average approximation, the Feb-2022 window extension, the synthetic catalog draws, and the unverified truth fixtures.
