Reading the page: the train is every Earth-relevant DONKI CME launched inside the membership window plus anything still in transit, modeled as ONE system with CME–CME interaction on (spec §16: followers ride their leader's wake, leaders take a dynamic rear squeeze, follower shocks run against the wake, not fresh wind — the physics validated on the May 2024 Gannon train). What this is

CMEs rarely travel alone — active regions erupt in salvos, and the worst storms (May 2024 Gannon, the 2003 Halloween storms) are compound events: a follower rope catches its leader's wake, squeezes it, and deepens the southward field beyond what either eruption could do alone. This page models the CURRENT state of the Sun–Earth corridor as one interacting flux-rope train, live from the DONKI catalog, continuously corrected by L1 data.

Compared to the Simulator

The Flux Rope Simulator is the parameter sandbox — hand-fit hindcasts, sliders, STEREO-A conditioning. This page is the operational view of the same Rust/WASM engine: no sliders, data decides. Physics and validation pins: spec · deep review.

Why measure the noise

A forecast of −6 nT means nothing if the ambient field wanders ±5 nT on a quiet day. The background band is measured live (median-absolute-deviation, storm-robust), shown on the chart, and fed into the model where it matters: the sheath's ambient variability seed and the particle filter's observation error — both previously fixed constants, both now disclosed on-page.
