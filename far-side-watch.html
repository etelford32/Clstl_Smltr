The clock moves the observer, not the data. The phase-shift field is held at the latest observation — active regions are pinned in Carrington longitude, which is the whole reason a far-side detection can be projected forward — while the sub-Earth point sweeps past them at the synodic rate. Scrub forward and a region reaches the east limb exactly when its lead time reads zero. Nothing here re-observes the Sun: a simulated epoch never fires an alert or changes the exported forecast.
