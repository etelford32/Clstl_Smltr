Phase 1 physics
① Metric: Kerr in Boyer-Lindquist (Σ = r²+a²cos²θ, Δ = r²−2r+a², A = (r²+a²)²−a²Δsin²θ). Reduces continuously to Schwarzschild at a = 0.
② Geodesics: Hamilton's equations on (xμ, pμ) — pt and pφ exactly conserved (Killing vectors). ∂rH, ∂θH by central difference.
③ Tetrad: ZAMO frame (Bardeen- Press-Teukolsky 1972) — locally non-rotating, valid through the ergosphere; Keplerian boost in the equatorial plane.
④ Termination: horizon capture at r ≲ r+(a) = 1+√(1−a²), or escape past rfar = 1200 M.
⑤ Disk: spin-correct Kerr Keplerian ΩK = 1/(r3/2+a) drives the Doppler asymmetry; ISCO snaps to BPT-1972 prograde value.
⑥ Inspector: click any pixel to re-run the geodesic in JS and read off E, Lz, Carter Q, their drift, b, rmin, total Δφ.
UI note: TON 618 mass is measured (virial/SED); high spin a ≈ 0.95 is inferred; super-Eddington phases are hypothetical.
