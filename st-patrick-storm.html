Why this storm matters

The community benchmark

17 March 2015 is the most-modeled storm in the magnetosphere literature — SWMF, LFM, and OpenGGCM all have published runs against it. Reproducing it under the same frozen configuration that ran Gannon is what turns one good run into a validation database.

The controlled comparison

Same grid, same solver ladder, same coupling cadences, same container image as the Gannon baseline (hc-std-v1) — only the storm changed. Peak ap 179 vs Gannon's pinned 400: this event tests the physics in the regime where the index still works.

Methodology

OMNI HRO 1-min L1 IMF is replayed into BATS-R-US + Ridley (SWMF) under the frozen hc-std-v1 configuration — the identical grid, three-session cold-start ladder, Boris correction, and coupling cadences as every other event in the database — yielding ΦPC and HPI at 5-minute cadence. Observed SYM-H/AE come from the committed OMNI ground-mag fixture (fingerprint-checked: −234 nT at 22:47 UT). Ap* applies the joint two-event pseudo-Ap fit (Gannon + Feb 2022) out of sample — its miss is data, not decoration.
